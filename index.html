How this works

  • Each card is one proposed change.
  • Vote 👍 Support, 🤔 Unsure / needs changes, or 👎 Oppose. You get one vote per card, and you can change it any time.
  • Cards with the most support float to the top. The order updates every minute as neighbors vote, but your own vote won't make cards jump while you're using the page.
  • Labels show who proposed each idea: SFMTA proposal SFCTA proposal Neighbor proposal
  • Cards with an orange border are outside the Bosworth/Monterey Quick-Build's stated area, but neighbors have asked about them. Votes there show they need their own attention.
  • Pictures come from the SFMTA and SFCTA presentations to the Sunnyside Neighborhood Association on Nov 9, 2026. Swipe or use the arrows to see more, and tap a picture to open it full size.

Community agreements

  1. Stay on topic: street safety in and around Sunnyside.
  2. Talk about the street, not people. Please avoid personal mentions/attacks.
  3. Be specific: where it is, and what happens there.
  4. No ads, links, or personal info (yours or anyone else's).
  5. Vote for as many proposals as you want, but please only submit one vote per proposal.

Comments that pass basic checks are posted right away; anything flagged by 3 neighbors is hidden until it's reviewed. New issue reports are reviewed by an organizer before they become cards.
