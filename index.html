Sunnyside · Monterey / Circular / I-280

Which street safety changes do you want?

The SFMTA and SFCTA have proposed changes around Monterey, Circular, Bosworth and the I-280 ramps, and neighbors have added their own ideas. Vote on each one, leave a comment, or report a problem spot we missed. The results will be shared with the project team.

How this works

Each card is one proposed change. Vote 👍 Support, 🤔 Unsure / needs changes, or 👎 Oppose. You get one vote per card and can change it any time. Cards with the most support float to the top, and the order updates every minute as neighbors vote. Your own vote won't make cards jump while you're using the page.

Community agreements

  1. Stay on topic: street safety in and around Sunnyside.
  2. Talk about the street, not people. No personal attacks.
  3. Be specific: where it is, and what happens there.
  4. No ads, links, or personal info (yours or anyone else's).
  5. One vote per person.

Comments that pass basic checks are posted right away; anything flagged by 3 neighbors is hidden until it's reviewed. New issue reports are reviewed by an organizer before they become cards.
